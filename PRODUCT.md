# IAYA UK

Iraqi Academic Youth Association UK: an independent, voluntary, non-profit organisation founded in 2019. Audience: Iraqi undergraduate/postgraduate students, young researchers and academics in the UK, academic partners and community members. Preserve the provided logo, committee names/portraits, association purpose and motto: “With knowledge and work, we build the future” / «بالعلم والعمل نبني المستقبل».

Public visitors can learn about the association, activities, committee, documented June 2026 event, verified news, resources and contact channels. Community members sign in with Google, submit an application, wait for committee approval and access member resources only after approval. Committee administrators review pending requests through server-authorized tools. No invented testimonials, event dates, members, metrics or directory entries.
