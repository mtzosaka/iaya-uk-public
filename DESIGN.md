# IAYA UK visual direction

Contemporary academic editorial design. Cream paper, deep navy ink, restrained warm-gold accent. The original logo remains unchanged. Large confident serif English headlines; readable Arabic and body type. Asymmetric first viewport uses real provided keynote photography, not invented association imagery. No arch-card wallpaper, repeated generic feature cards, or decorative dashboards.

Semantic colors: background #faf7ef, surface #ffffff, text #122b42, secondary #526578, accent #795500. Dark mode: background #081b30, surface #102840, text #f9f5e9, secondary #bbc8d6, accent #e8c980. Primary controls deep blue with cream in light mode, warm gold with deep blue in dark. Body 17px; measure 65ch. Display maximum 88px; tracking no tighter than -.035em. Spacing steps 4/8/12/16/24/32/48/64/96/128px.

Editorial rows and unboxed portraits replace repetitive cards. Header collapses below 1024px. The member flow uses clearer compact controls and consistent forms rather than expressive marketing treatments. Persist only language/theme preferences in browser storage. Auth and membership are server-managed.

One authored entrance on the hero image, visible by default. Interaction transitions <=220ms, cubic-bezier(.23,1,.32,1), press scale(.97), reduced-motion support. No scroll hijacking or endless animation. Apply design guidance from the three user-provided repos; retain license/provenance in docs/design-sources.
